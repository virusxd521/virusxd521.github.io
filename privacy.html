fetch-digest privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Who uses it

fetch-digest is a personal script. Only its owner (GitHub user virusxd521) uses it, with the owner's own Google account.

What Google data it reads

  • Gmail messages, with the read-only scope gmail.readonly.
  • Calendar events, with the read-only scope calendar.readonly.

It cannot send, delete or change mail or events.

How it uses the data

It makes a short text summary of new mail and of today's and tomorrow's events, for the owner's own daily review. The owner reads the summary, also with an AI assistant that the owner uses under the owner's own account.

Where the data stays

The access key and the summaries stay on the owner's computer. Summaries are deleted after 7 days. There is no server and no cloud database.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not used for ads and not given to any other person or company.

How to stop access

Remove fetch-digest at myaccount.google.com/connections, or delete the key file on the computer.

Contact

Through GitHub: github.com/virusxd521.
